## Getting started

### Install Consilia and use it offline

Consilia is a web app. Open the link once with internet, and it saves itself on your device. After that it works with no signal, including the whole KSSR 2017 curriculum.

1. Open the Consilia link in Chrome (Android, laptop) or Safari (iPhone, iPad).
2. Android and laptop: tap Install in Me → Storage, or "Add to Home screen" in the browser menu.
3. iPhone and iPad: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
4. Wait for the green "Ready to work offline" chip in Me.

> Tip: When an update is ready, Consilia asks before switching, so a lesson in Teach mode is never interrupted.

### Set up your profile

The first screen asks for your language, name, practicum school, school type (SK or SJK) and programme. Your name and school appear on printed lesson plans. Change them any time in Me → Profile.

### Take the guided tour

An 8-step tour shows the main screens the first time you use Consilia. Replay it from Me → Help → Replay the guided tour.

### Find your way around

Today: your next lesson, today's periods, the week as a route, what is coming up, your to-do list and your tools. Plan: every lesson plan, My day, the weekly timetable and your yearly plan. Classes: Year 1 to Year 6 classes, pupils and PBD. Library: the SoW, DSKP, Method Guide, activity bank and your textbooks. Tools: every tool, offline and AI. Me: settings, backup, help, privacy and feedback.

Create (the gold tile on a laptop, the round + button on a phone) opens the ten things teachers start most often: a new lesson plan, Let Consilia plan it, the Lesson plan writer, a quiz, a reading text, a worksheet, a game for the projector, attendance, My day and a message to parents.

Search (the magnifier, or Ctrl+K or / on a keyboard) finds tools, your lesson plans and classes, every lesson in the Scheme of Work for Years 1 to 6, and the help articles. Enter opens the first result; Esc closes Search.

## Planning a lesson

### Plan your first lesson

Plan → New lesson plan. Three short questions set up the plan, then the builder walks you through seven steps. Everything saves as you type.

1. Class and time: choose your class (or type one), the date, start time and length.
2. How do you want to start? Pick one of four ways (see below).
3. Choose the SoW lesson, then press Create lesson plan.

> Tip: The score ring at the top shows how well the plan is aligned. Tap it to see what to fix.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Four ways to start

Consilia recommends: tell Consilia about your class and it plans the whole lesson for you (see "Let Consilia plan a lesson").

Follow the SoW: everything from the SoW lesson is filled in (standards, outline steps and materials). Improve and expand it.

SoW standards, my activities: the Content Standard, Learning Standards, skills and theme come from the SoW, and you design the activities.

From scratch: choose your own standards from the DSKP and plan everything yourself.

### Step 1 · Setup

Check the class name, level, number of pupils, time, theme and topic. Cross-curricular element (CCE) and moral values have suggestions from the SoW. "What pupils already know" can be filled from your last reflection for the class.

### Step 2 · Standards

The main and complementary skills with their Content and Learning Standards. In the SoW modes they are locked to the SoW; press "Change them" if you really need different ones.

### Step 3 · Objectives with ABCD and SMART

Every objective is built from four boxes: Audience (who), Behaviour (an action you can see), Condition (with what, or how) and Degree (how well, as a number).

The S, M, A, R and T squares turn green when the objective is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound. Each red square says what to change, for example "Add a number to the Degree, e.g. at least 4 out of 5".

Success criteria are written for pupils ("I can…") and update from your objective.

> Tip: "Understand", "know" and "learn" cannot be observed, so Consilia suggests verbs such as identify, match, say or write.
>
> Tip: "With minimal support" is not a measurable Degree. Use a number: "at least 3 of 5 sentences".

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Step 4 · Lesson flow and frameworks (PPP, PWP and more)

Consilia suggests a framework from the main skill and explains why: PWP for listening and reading, PPP for a language point or speaking phrase, Process Writing for writing in Years 3–6, Language Arts for songs, rhymes and stories, and Task-based for fluency in Years 4–6. You can choose another one.

Each stage has activities with what the teacher does, what pupils do, the interaction and teaching aids. Low, moderate and high tiers show how each activity is differentiated.

The timing bar adds up your minutes. "Reset to suggested times" shares the lesson length across the stages.

### Step 5 · Materials, Canva AI and links

List the teaching aids, key vocabulary and appendices.

Textbook: the book and pages for the lesson, filled in from the Scheme of Work. Add a page or a range (e.g. Student's Book p. 56 to 57) or remove one. They are printed on the lesson plan and in the Word file ("Textbook: Get Smart Plus 4 · Student's Book p. 56–57") and open in Teach mode. If the book is on your Textbook Shelf, tick "Print pictures of these pages" to print the pages themselves after the plan, and in the Word file.

Design with Canva AI: Consilia writes a prompt from your lesson. Press Copy prompt, then Open Canva AI and paste it.

Find ready-made materials opens searches on Wordwall, Liveworksheets, Quizizz, Kahoot and YouTube. Add any link you use to the lesson; it gets a QR code on the printed lesson plans.

The Materials studio makes printables from your lesson: flashcards, gap-fill and matching worksheets, sentence strips, exit tickets, tiered worksheets and an assessment rubric. Tick "In lesson plan appendix" to print them with the plan.

### Step 6 · Plan B

Plan for what often goes wrong: pupils finish early, the lesson runs late, no projector or electricity, a noisy class, many pupils absent. "Suggest for empty cards" fills ideas you can edit.

### Step 7 · Review, print and Word

The alignment score checks that objectives, activities, assessment and timing all match. Each item says what is wrong and jumps to the fix. If something is fine for your context, press Mark as fine and add a note.

Choose a layout: School template (the standard lesson plan format) or Consilia Lesson Blueprint (a one-page visual plan).

Print or save as PDF, or Download Word (.docx) to edit in Word, Google Docs or WPS.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Weekly planner and timetable

Plan → This week. Set up your English timetable once (day, time, class, minutes). Each slot shows whether the lesson is planned, and "Plan this lesson" starts the lesson plan with the class and time filled in.

For sports day or a holiday, choose "No class this day…" and Mark no class. Undo brings it back.

### My day: arrange your day

Plan → My day (or My day on Today) shows one day: your English periods from the timetable, lesson plans dated that day, and anything you add yourself, such as a relief class, a meeting or marking.

Drag items, or use ↑ ↓, to put them in the order you want, and tick them off as you go. Each period has Plan this lesson, Teach and Attendance. Notes for the day are kept with it.

> Tip: If you have a yearly plan, My day shows what it says for this week.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Yearly, monthly and weekly plans

Plan → Yearly plan. Upload your school's yearly plan (Rancangan Pengajaran Tahunan) as a Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), CSV or PDF file made from Word. Consilia reads the table on this device: weeks, dates, themes and topics, Learning Standards, SoW lesson numbers and holiday or exam weeks, in Malay or English.

Check the result, correct anything, choose the classes that follow it and save. No file? Choose "Build it from the Scheme of Work": Consilia lays out the SoW lessons week by week, skipping the holiday weeks you list.

Yearly shows every week with this week highlighted. Monthly shows the weeks as a calendar. Weekly shows the week's topic, standards and SoW lessons, and matches them to your timetable periods: "Plan lesson 12" opens New lesson plan with the lesson, class and time already chosen.

> Tip: Old .doc or .xls files: open them and "Save as" .docx or .xlsx first.
>
> Tip: A scanned PDF has no text to read: upload the Word or Excel version instead.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Copy, duplicate, share and delete lessons

In Plan, each lesson has Duplicate (for another class), Copy, Share and Delete.

Share makes a .consilia file you can send on WhatsApp or Drive. It never includes your class or pupil names. A friend opens it with "Import a shared lesson (.consilia)".

## Consilia Recommends and Tools

### Let Consilia plan a lesson

Plan → New lesson plan → Consilia recommends (or "Let Consilia plan it" on Today). Choose the class, date and SoW lesson, then answer a few questions about your class: ability, what the pupils are like, what your classroom has (projector, speakers, internet, devices), your key words and target sentence, what matters today, and how much time you have to prepare.

Consilia then plans the whole lesson on your device and shows how it decided: the framework and why, one or two activities for every stage with a step-by-step script, Low / Moderate / High versions, teaching aids with pictures and making steps, a digital option that suits your classroom, SMART objectives, assessment and Plan B.

Nothing is sent anywhere: Consilia's planning engine uses the KSSR data, the framework rules and more than 80 activities written for primary English.

1. Answer the questions; Consilia remembers them for that class.
2. Read the plan. Press ↻ Another idea on any stage you want to change; the other stages stay as they are.
3. Tick the teaching aids you already have in the preparation checklist.
4. Press Use this plan. It opens in the builder, where you can change anything.

> Tip: Choose "Observation lesson" as a goal for activities with a memorable moment.
>
> Tip: Without internet, Consilia only suggests activities and digital options that work offline.
>
> Tip: The plan is a starting point: adapt it to your pupils and check it with your mentor.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### The Tools page

Tools collects everything besides the lesson plan builder, in seven groups: Plan, Create materials, Assess, Support every pupil, In the classroom, Fun and creative, and Write for me. Twelve tools work offline (Consilia recommends, Activity ideas, Objective writer, Differentiation helper, Teaching aids, Worksheet maker, Chant maker, Lesson slides, AI prompt builder, Classroom games, Digital tools and Classroom management); 24 more write with AI (see the next article). Tap the star on a tool to keep it in Favourites.

1. Tell the tools your lesson once: in the bar at the top press "Use my latest lesson plan", choose one of your lesson plans, or type the topic and key words. Every tool fills itself in from it.
2. Use a tool. Results already use your year, topic, words and sentence.
3. Press "＋ Add to my lesson" (activity ideas, chants) or "Use as main objective" to put the result straight into your lesson plan.

> Tip: In the lesson plan builder, "Tools for this step" opens the right tools already filled with that lesson.
>
> Tip: Search the Tools page by what you need, in English or Malay: "warm-up", "kad imbas", "noisy class", "lagu".
>
> Tip: New to a tool? Press "Try an example" to see it working before you type anything.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### AI tools: write it for me

24 tools are marked AI: Lesson plan writer, Lesson hooks, Real-life connections, Activity designer, Reading and listening texts, Text leveller, Vocabulary builder, Role-play and dialogue, Phonics pack, Grammar explainer, Quiz maker, Questions at every level, PBD rubric, Exit tickets, Writing feedback, Sentence frames and word banks, Help for one pupil, Song and rhyme writer, Choose-your-path story, Riddles and jokes, Message to parents, Lesson reflection, Professional email and Ask the teaching assistant. Each writes for Malaysian primary English (KSSR, CEFR) at the year you choose.

With Claude or Gemini connected in Me → AI assistant, the result appears as it is written. Without it, the main button copies the prompt: paste it into Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, then paste the answer back. Only what you type in the form is sent, and only when you press the button; Consilia first checks the text for your pupils' names and offers to remove them.

Base it on a KSSR lesson: under Year, open "Base it on a KSSR lesson" and choose a lesson from the Scheme of Work. The form fills itself from the lesson, and Consilia sends the AI that lesson's real learning standards (codes and wording), theme, language focus, textbook pages and the Scheme of Work's own steps, so the answer follows your RPT instead of guessing. A tool opened for a lesson plan that follows the Scheme of Work is based on the same lesson already.

Every result gets a Quality check: the sections asked for, the numbers (8 questions, 5 ideas, stages that add up to the lesson's minutes, TP1 to TP6), measurable objectives and "I can" success criteria, learning standards that really exist for the year (or match the Scheme of Work lesson), sentences short enough for the year's CEFR level, British spelling, and blanks such as [Your name] to fill in. Press "Fix it" on a problem, or "Fix all", and the AI sends a corrected version.

1. Open Tools and choose a tool. Use the groups, search, or the star to keep favourites at the top.
2. Fill in the form, or press "Try an example". The year, topic and key words come from your lesson bar.
3. Press "Generate with Claude" (or "Copy the prompt", then paste the answer back).
4. Change it with one tap ("Make it simpler", "Add Malay meanings for the key words" …) or type what to change. Each change is a new version; go back with the arrows. You can also edit it by hand.
5. Use it: Copy (headings and tables stay in Word, Google Docs or WhatsApp), Print, Word, Read aloud, Projector, Play as a Quiz show, Spin wheel, Open in the lesson plan builder or Add to my lesson plan.

> Tip: Every result is kept in Tools → My creations with all its versions, and goes into your backups.
>
> Tip: The Lesson plan writer's "Open in the lesson plan builder" makes a real lesson plan. Only learning standards that exist in KSSR are kept, so check the Standards step.
>
> Tip: Check every AI result before pupils see it: AI can make mistakes. The quality check catches missing parts and the wrong level, not every wrong fact.
>
> Tip: Never type pupils' names. If you do, Consilia stops and offers to replace them with "the pupil".

### Classroom games on the projector

Tools → Classroom games, or the Games tool in Teach mode (filled with the lesson's key words). Spin wheel picks a word or question; Unjumble mixes your sentences; Mystery word reveals a word letter by letter; Quiz show keeps team scores. Press Full screen for the projector. The words come from the lesson bar. With a keyboard or a presentation clicker: Space spins the wheel, shows the answer or the next letter; → goes to the next item.

### Teaching aids step by step

Tools → Teaching aids shows 25 cheap teaching aids with a picture, what you need, how to make them step by step, how to use them in class, a time and cost estimate, and safety notes. Filter by use (vocabulary, phonics, stories, assessment…) or "Ready in 10 minutes".

### Lesson slides, PowerPoint, Canva and Google Slides

Open a lesson plan's Materials step and tap Make lesson slides (also Slides in the Plan list, Lesson slides on the Review step, or Tools → Lesson slides). Choose a look. Consilia makes a full deck from the plan on your device: the topic, "I can" goals, warm-up, key words, the target sentence with examples, one slide per activity with pupil steps, the pattern (pairs, groups) and minutes, quick-check questions with answers, a thumbs check and an exit ticket. Your teacher notes are on every slide. Steps written only for the teacher (for example "Follow the Teacher's Book") stay in the notes.

Edit any slide (title, lines, answer, notes), move it, duplicate it, delete it or add a new one. Changes are saved with the lesson plan and in backups. "Make again from the plan" starts over after you change the plan.

Present: Present, or the Slides tool in Teach mode, which opens at the current stage. → or Space or a presentation clicker goes on (a question shows its answer first), ← goes back, F is full screen, N shows your notes, Esc stops.

Download PowerPoint (.pptx) works offline. Open it in PowerPoint, or import it into Canva (Create a design → Import file) or Google Slides (upload to Drive, or File → Import slides) to add pictures and animations. On a phone you can send the file straight to the Canva or Google Slides app.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Improve with AI

Consilia writes careful prompts for your pupils' level (Year and CEFR) that you paste into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or Canva AI: reading or listening texts, comprehension questions, dialogues, worksheets in three levels, songs, flashcard pictures, stories, rubrics, or feedback on your whole plan.

Without the AI assistant, Consilia never sends anything itself, and prompts never contain pupils' names. AI answers can be wrong: check them before using them with pupils, and follow your institute's rules on AI.

For slides, copy the slide prompt, paste it into any AI chat (such as the free Claude.ai), then paste the answer into "Paste the AI's answer here": a reading text, dialogue, word meanings, examples and questions are added as slides marked AI.

### AI assistant: Claude (or Gemini) with your own key

Consilia works fully without AI. If you want answers inside the app, go to Me → AI assistant. Claude (by Anthropic) is recommended: in the Claude Console add a little credit under Billing (you can set a spend limit), create an API key and paste it. A slide request costs a few sen. Gemini (by Google) is the free alternative, with a key from Google AI Studio. Tap Test the key (for Claude you can choose Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 or Haiku 5.5), read and tick the three agreements, then turn it on.

Then "Add with Claude" on Lesson slides writes a reading or listening text, a dialogue, questions, word meanings or more examples, and places them in the deck (marked AI; "Remove AI slides" takes them out). In the AI prompt builder and Improve with AI, "ask Claude here" shows the answer in Consilia.

What is sent: the lesson's year, topic, theme, skill, key words, target sentence and objectives, or the prompt you can see. Never pupils' names, class names, marks, attendance or your details. Keys stay in this browser only (not in backups or shared lessons); each AI keeps its own key. It needs the internet. Turn it off or remove the keys in Me → AI assistant. No key? Every AI button also copies the prompt for the free Claude.ai chat.

### Terms of use

Before first use, every teacher agrees that Consilia is a planning tool: you stay responsible for your lessons, check suggestions and websites before using them, and protect pupils' privacy. Read them again in Me → Terms of use.

## Teaching with Consilia

### Teach mode

Press Teach on any lesson. Teach mode is full screen and large enough to project.

1. Take attendance: tap the pupils who are absent.
2. Start the lesson. "Today we will…" shows the success criteria for the class.
3. Use Next and Back to move through the stages. The clock shows time left in each stage and warns gently when you are running over.
4. At the end, choose Record PBD for this lesson or Write my reflection.

> Tip: If the page reloads or the phone locks, Teach mode resumes where you were.
>
> Tip: Press and hold Exit to leave, so a pupil cannot close it by accident.
>
> Tip: Background music always pauses in Teach mode.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Classroom toolkit

Name picker: picks a present pupil at random and does not repeat until "Start a new round".

Groups: mixed ability or by level, and it respects "Keep apart" and "Keep together" pairs from pupil profiles.

Noise: uses the microphone to show a green, amber or red light. Nothing is recorded. If the microphone is not allowed, tap the colours yourself.

Points: +1 or − for groups. Attention: a 3-2-1 signal. Timer: quick countdowns. Textbook: opens the pages linked to the lesson. Games: the projector games, filled with the lesson's key words.

### Projecting a textbook page

In the textbook viewer, use zoom, Pen, Highlighter, Spotlight (focus on one part of the page) and Answer cover (drag to reveal answers). Clear ink removes your marks.

### Reflect after the lesson

Write my reflection asks what happened, what worked and what to change, with prompts from your lesson data (time spent per stage, PBD results). Your next lesson for the class can start from it.

## Classes, pupils and PBD

### Add classes and pupils

Classes → choose Year 1 to Year 6 → Add class. Give it a name ("4 Zhong"), school type (SK or SJK) and an optional level.

Open the class and paste names, one per line, from Excel, Google Sheets or WhatsApp. Numbers and bullets are removed for you, and duplicate names get a letter (Ali A., Ali B.).

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Take attendance

Classes → your class → Take attendance (or Attendance in My day). Tap P (present), L (late), A (absent) or E (excused, such as MC or school duty) for each pupil, add a reason if you like, then "Everyone else present" marks the rest in one tap. It saves as you go, for any date.

Month shows every day recorded with each pupil's attendance rate (below 80% is highlighted) and exports to Excel. Copy summary gives a short text for the class teacher. Absences you mark in Teach mode are added too, without changing marks you set.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Record PBD (TP1–TP6)

Record TP levels from a class, or from the end of Teach mode. Choose the Learning Standard, then tap TP1 to TP6 for each pupil. "Set everyone to" fills a level for all, then you change the few who differ.

"What each TP means" shows the official descriptor for that skill and year.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### PBD progress and Excel export

PBD progress shows a heatmap of every pupil and Learning Standard, a suggested overall TP, and suggested groups for differentiation.

Export to Excel makes a spreadsheet for your school records.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### Pupil profiles

Tap a pupil to see their TP history, write a private note (protected by your PIN if set), and set Keep apart or Keep together pairs for grouping.

## Library and textbooks

### SoW, DSKP and standards

Library holds the full KSSR 2017 Scheme of Work (SK and SJK, Years 1–6), the Learning Standards and TP descriptors. Search everything finds SoW lessons, Learning Standards, SoW tasks, differentiation strategies, activities and Method Guide pages for the year at once.

KSSR 2027 is labelled "stay tuned" until the ministry publishes it.

### Method Guide and activity bank

The Method Guide explains when to use PPP, PWP, Process Writing, Task-based learning and Language Arts, with examples.

The activity bank has about 70 activities by skill. Insert one into a lesson as a copy; editing it never changes the bank.

### Add your textbooks

Library → Textbooks shows the book for each year (Super Minds 1 for Years 1 and 2, Get Smart Plus 3, Get Smart Plus 4, English Plus 1 for Year 5, Academy Stars for Year 6) and which ones are on this device. Get your own copy from KPM: open DELIMa, sign in with your DELIMa ID, find the digital textbooks (Buku Teks Digital), choose the year and English, and download the book. Consilia links to KPM and does not host the books.

Then press Add PDF on the matching book and choose the file. It stays on this device only.

Consilia asks one quick question ("One quick check for this PDF") so printed page numbers match the PDF pages. Then you can open any page by number, unit or topic.

### Make a scanned textbook searchable

Many textbook PDFs are scans, so their words cannot be searched with "Search the books". On the Textbook Shelf, press Make searchable. Consilia reads each page on your device (nothing is uploaded) and you can keep using the app while it works. It takes a few minutes per book and only happens once.

The first time, about 7 MB of text recognition files are downloaded (with internet) and kept for offline use. Press Pause at any time; Continue picks up from the same page, even after closing the app.

## Your data, backup and privacy

### Back up and restore

Me → Backup → Back up now makes one file with your lessons, classes, pupils, PBD, reflections, settings and photos. Keep it in Google Drive, WhatsApp to yourself, or your laptop. Today reminds you each week.

To restore, press Restore from a file and choose it. "Add to what is here" adds the backup to what is already on this device; "Replace everything here" makes this device match the backup exactly.

Textbook PDFs are not in the backup because they are large. Add them again on a new device.

Video: Me → Help → Video tutorials.

### PIN lock for pupil data

Me → PIN lock for pupil data → Set a PIN. Pupil names and notes are encrypted on the device and in backups. The app locks after 5 minutes away, or press Lock now.

A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered by anyone. Remove PIN needs the current PIN.

### Privacy

Consilia has no server and no account. Everything stays on your device. Only feedback you choose to send leaves it. Read the full page in Me → Privacy, in English or Bahasa Melayu.

### Storage space

Me → Storage shows how much space Consilia uses. Textbooks use the most. If the device is nearly full, remove a textbook you are not using, then make a backup.

## Settings and more

### Language, theme and music

Me → choose English or Bahasa Melayu, and Light, Dark or System theme.

Background music has three original R&B instrumentals. It is off until you press play, and pauses when Consilia is in the background or in Teach mode.

### Mentor and lecturer feedback

Me → Mentor and lecturer feedback. Log comments after an observation and tag them. Patterns shows what keeps coming up, with links to the Method Guide.

### Send feedback in the Feedback Garden

Me → About and Feedback Garden. Choose a mood, the type (Idea, Bug, Praise or Question), write your message and vote for up to 3 ideas. Each message plants a flower. If you are offline, it is sent when you are back online.

### Troubleshooting

A screen looks old after an update: close Consilia fully and open it again, then accept the update prompt.

A textbook will not open: the PDF may have been removed by the browser to free space. Add it again on the Textbook Shelf.

Pupil names show "Locked": unlock with your PIN in Me.

Something else: tell the developer in the Feedback Garden and choose Bug.
